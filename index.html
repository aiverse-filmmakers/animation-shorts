THE RULE THAT PREVENTS LOST PROJECTS

When the AI says SAVE THIS, actually save it outside the chat.

Approved decisions and media must survive a new conversation, a new AI provider or next month's Episode 2. The Master is required to explain the exact filename, why it matters, when it is needed again and how to save it.

EPISODE-SAFE CONTINUITY

Episode 2 can have Shot 1 without colliding with Episode 1.

Recurring assets keep IDs such as CHAR-01, PROP-01 and WORLD-01. Editorial material uses episode-safe IDs such as E01-SHOT-01 and E02-SHOT-01. Grid panel numbers, shots, generation blocks and takes remain separate.

WHAT THE AI SHOULD DO FOR YOU

The research is intelligence, not homework.

The project researched award-winning animation so the AI can apply transferable story principles when developing your work. You should not be sent away to repeat that study unless you specifically want to learn it.

From almost nothing

Ask for three ideas, give one vague premise, supply an image or start with an existing script. The AI should create the missing story artifacts internally and surface only the decisions you need to approve.

Continuity automatically

The AI reads the approved script and decides which characters, props and worlds need locks. It writes the reference prompts and records approved files instead of asking you to invent a continuity system.

Production handoffs

Every time you leave the AI chat, you should know the next tool, exact uploads, exact prompt, number of attempts, success criteria, output filename and what to bring back.
