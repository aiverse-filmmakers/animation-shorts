Example idea

“A nervous teenage alien and a reckless inventor accidentally call cosmic customer support and the support agent keeps making their universe worse.”

1
Episode directionYou get the idea back as a clear episode, not hidden reasoning.
Make it about two minutes. Fast absurd sci-fi comedy.
EPISODE SUMMARY: A reckless inventor’s broken teleporter connects to Universal Reality Customer Support. A bored cosmic agent tries to “fix” the problem by reversing gravity, removing their bones, replacing the Moon and nearly deleting Earth before discovering Undo. Ending: Earth returns, but the teleporter is still broken and the call disconnects. NEXT: I’ll write the script.
Continuation updated: premise, summary, ending, tone and runtime are now saved.
2
ScriptThe script is written for you, but you do not have to read it unless you want to.
Script complete. SHORT SUMMARY: The call starts as a teleporter error, escalates through three catastrophic “fixes,” then ends on the broken teleporter gag. Want to read the full script here? Reply: SHOW SCRIPT NEXT: choose the animation look.
Samurai Jack-inspired 2D.
Continuation updated: full script is preserved even if it is not printed into the chat.
3
Animation lookThe Director locks the medium in a few useful words.
Locked: graphic 2D adult TV animation with bold angular shapes, flat cel shading, strong silhouettes, dramatic negative space and clean sci-fi environments. NEXT: I’ll define the recurring characters and world in words.
Saved: Animation Visual DNA. This text is reused in future prompts so the medium does not drift.
4
Text visual canonImportant characters, locations and props are locked in words before images.
CHAR-01 TEXT LOCK: Tall, lanky adult human inventor. Long angular face, heavy black brows, swept jagged black hair, white lab coat, black shirt, dark trousers. Impatient, sleep-deprived posture. CHAR-02 TEXT LOCK: Slim teenage alien with cyan-blue skin, oversized pointed ears, large readable eyes, dark spiky hair, cropped off-white jacket with olive shoulder panels, black shirt and dark trousers. WORLD-01 TEXT LOCK: Moonlit inventor’s lab with a cylindrical teleporter chamber, large mountain-facing windows, practical workbenches, warm task lights and cool blue moonlight. NEXT: create the master hero image.
Why this matters: future prompts reuse these exact descriptions. The visual canon exists in words and images.
5
Hero image promptThe Brain gives you the exact prompt. It does not silently generate a random design.
CREATE: HERO-01-LAB-CALL.png UPLOAD: None yet. PROMPT: [full hero prompt using the exact CHAR-01, CHAR-02 and WORLD-01 text locks] SAVE AS: HERO-01-LAB-CALL.png Generate it and bring the result back here.
[uploads generated hero image]
Hero approved. NEXT: turn the approved hero designs into proper reference sheets.
Saved: hero source prompt + approved hero filename.
6
Reference sheetsEach sheet uses the hero image plus the exact text lock.
CREATE: CHAR-01-INVENTOR.png UPLOAD: 1. HERO-01-LAB-CALL.png ROLE: visual authority for the inventor’s approved design. PROMPT: [character sheet prompt] CHAR-01 TEXT LOCK: [the exact same inventor description used in the hero prompt] SAVE AS: CHAR-01-INVENTOR.png
The Director repeats this only for assets that genuinely need their own continuity reference: recurring characters, important locations, props or products.
Each locked asset keeps three things: TEXT LOCK + IMAGE LOCK + SOURCE PROMPT.
7
Video constraintsBefore shot planning, the Director asks what your video tool can actually generate.
What is the maximum duration your video generator can create per generation? 5 sec / 10 sec / 15 sec / 30 sec / other
10 seconds.
Locked: maximum generation unit = 10 seconds. NEXT: I’ll build Production Board 01 around that limit.
Rule: the Director does not design a 30-second generation prompt for a tool that only makes 10-second clips.
8
Production Board 01No giant two-minute storyboard. Each board is small enough to use.
PRODUCTION BOARD 01 00:00–00:24 · 4 shots 01 · 6s · Inventor works on teleporter 02 · 6s · Portal becomes support interface 03 · 6s · Support worker connects 04 · 6s · Gravity “fix” is triggered Board limit: max 30 seconds / max 8 shots. NEXT: create Video 01 only.
Continuation updated: Board 01 is recorded. Later boards wait until needed.

[uploads V01-START.png]
Start frame approved. VIDEO PROMPT: [exact 6-second motion prompt] UPLOAD: 1. V01-START.png — exact opening composition 2. character/location refs only if the model supports them and they help SAVE AS: V01.mp4 Generate it and bring it back.

After every approved clip: the latest continuation state can restart the project in another chat.
11
Audio, edit and finishOnly after the footage exists.
All required clips are approved. NEXT: 1. dialogue / VO if needed 2. ambience + SFX 3. final edit 4. final QC I’ll keep the same one-step-at-a-time workflow.
