# AI Animation Shorts Brain System: Source Extraction and Architecture

LAST REVIEWED: 2026-09-04

## Award-winning story findings

The public award and craft sources in this repository were used as a filter, not as a formula. The reusable pattern is emotional clarity under constraint. A short needs a readable want, a visible obstacle, escalating attempts, a genuine turn, a changed choice and an ending image that pays off what the opening established. Jury language repeatedly values emotional impact, originality, clarity, surprise, poignancy and visual storytelling. Awards also recognise varied forms, including experimental work, first films, music and audience response. The system therefore asks what the audience feels and understands, not whether a film resembles a winning title.

## AI-Verse method extracted from SPRINT 2

1. Stop prompting and start directing. Important elements are cast once and then protected.
2. Read the script and extract recurring or high-risk elements before generating the visual package.
3. Use character, animal, prop, product, vehicle and environment reference sheets only when they materially improve continuity.
4. Preserve the tested reference layouts. Character and animal sheets commonly use front full body, profile full body, front face and profile face. Props and products adapt the views to their important construction and material details.
5. Build a hero image with the approved references before making the storyboard.
6. Think visually with a grid. Scene Imagination explores development, Freeze Multi-Angle preserves one moment while changing viewpoint, Story Progression shows beginning → build → shift → resolution, and A → B Bridge fills a logical transition between known states.
7. Number grid panels and use the simple extraction instruction for approved frames. Selection comes before animation.
8. Commercial Ready storyboard logic remains useful for production handoff: scene/time, shot/camera/movement, frame/composition, action/dialogue and notes/audio. Its rendering language must adapt to the chosen animation medium.
9. Prefer roughly five shots in a controlled storyboard or generation pass, allowing 5-8 when the material stays readable. This is a control heuristic, not a model law.
10. Use 30-second sections as story and production containers. Divide them into actual shots and model-sized generation blocks.
11. Use simple motion language when it is enough. Granular detail is for meaningful control, not decorative prompt length.
12. For independently generated clips, default to `Audio: no music` when continuous music will be added later, while retaining ambience, effects and dialogue. Permit native music as an explicit decision.
13. Generate → compare → select → lock. Do not automatically promote a failed generation into a new reference.

## Visual Architect architecture extracted

The supplied Visual Architect source demonstrates a useful public-safe architecture: a role has a purpose, capabilities, operating rules, structured inputs, output formats, continuity locks, granular controls and quality gates. The new brains use those concepts in smaller files. They do not reproduce private system instructions, hidden policies, credentials, proprietary routing, or unrelated tool mechanics. Complexity is used only where it reduces ambiguity.

## Final architecture

Each canonical brain is independent. It contains a role, purpose, compressed Story DNA, inputs or operating logic, a copyable output contract, handoff and quality checks. The Master Director adds progressive project memory with PROPOSED, APPROVED and LOCKED states. Specialist files do not require the Master file.

The pipeline is deliberately separated into four layers: film, scenes/sequences, editorial shots and generation blocks. This prevents a 30-second story section from being mistaken for one 30-second generation and prevents a model's multi-shot feature from replacing editorial thinking.

## Current AI filmmaking knowledge

Current workflow notes are retained in dated research documents. Stable principles are kept in the brains. Model-specific capabilities, access, duration, references, audio and API details are modular, dated and labelled verified, researched or to verify. This keeps the brains useful when models change.

## Validation decisions

The system must prove that:

1. Every major brain works when downloaded alone.
2. The Consistency Director extracts and ranks recurring elements from a script instead of asking the beginner to do it manually.
3. The Storyboard Director selects among the five AI-Verse modes and preserves the animation medium.
4. Shot IDs, grid numbers and generation-block IDs remain distinct.
5. The packager covers the runtime with no gaps or overlaps.
6. The Motion Director chooses single-frame, start/end, reference or multi-reference routes without inventing attachment tags.
7. Audio continuity is handled as a reasoned rule, not a blind prohibition.
8. The final critic evaluates story and technical integrity together.
9. The Master asks progressive questions and maintains approved state.
10. No file claims a route is tested unless the exact route was actually run.
