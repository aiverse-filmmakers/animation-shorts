# AI Film Workflow Research

**Research date:** 4 September 2026

This note records the public practices used to shape the downloadable AI Animation Shorts brains. It is a workflow reference, not a claim that every platform has the same controls.

## Repeated patterns found

### 1. Start with a story object, not a giant prompt

Current creative platforms commonly support starting from a concept, script, image or video. LTX Studio describes paths from a script, concept, image or video, then turns that starting point into scenes and storyboards.[1] The practical lesson is to decide the story question, character, action and ending before asking an AI system to generate many shots.

### 2. Build reusable visual ingredients

Google Flow describes creating characters or scene ingredients, then reusing the same ingredients across clips and scenes for consistency.[2] Its help documentation also describes adding visual references and using start and end frames to guide a transition.[3] The brains therefore ask the user to create a character lock, world lock and key-object list before generating a sequence.

### 3. Work in short shots and iterate

Flow exposes generation length, multiple outputs, start and end frames, and follow-up refinement.[3] Its scene tools let creators arrange clips, extend action and keep previous versions in an asset history.[4] The practical lesson is to generate short, reviewable units, keep rejected versions separate, and only continue after the current shot passes the story and continuity check.

### 4. Use prompts as visual language

In Adobe's account of its 2025 Generative AI Film Festival, director Dave Clark described beginning with detailed prompts as visual language for the world, then combining that with a conventional filmmaking workflow.[5] This supports the course rule that prompts should describe the intended visual decision, not replace story decisions.

### 5. Human selection remains the directing step

The same Adobe report frames AI as a toolbox while keeping human creativity at the centre of choices.[5] The public creator interviews around the 2025 Shortest AI Film Festival similarly describe AI as useful for early colour testing and idea generation, while personal taste, references and selection remain central.[6] The brains therefore require variation, rejection criteria and a deliberate keeper choice rather than automatic acceptance of the first output.

### 6. Sound is a separate design pass

Adobe's 2025 Firefly update describes generating custom sound effects inside the creative workflow and treats image, video and sound as separate creative capabilities.[7] The sound brain therefore asks for ambience, Foley, voice and music decisions after the picture structure is stable, instead of hiding sound inside an overloaded visual prompt.

### 7. Continuity is a production system, not a lucky prompt

The official platform examples repeatedly use saved assets, ingredients, frames, history and scene organization.[2][3][4] The portable method is to maintain a small continuity sheet containing character appearance, costume, props, setting, time, lighting, animation style, screen direction and the approved reference image for each recurring element.

## How these findings changed the download pack

1. The master brain routes the task through story, design, generation, review and finishing.
2. The synopsis brain forces a dramatic question, want, obstacle, turn and ending.
3. The script brain separates action, dialogue, sound and visual intention.
4. The 30-second planner separates story chunks from tool-specific generation limits. A 30-second story unit can contain several shorter generation shots if the chosen tool needs that.
5. The continuity brain treats references and saved frames as working assets, never as magic identity guarantees.
6. The storyboard brain creates one clear job per shot and a cut reason for every transition.
7. The animation director brain outputs a reusable shot contract with render, look, grade, audio, voice and motion fields, while avoiding unconfirmed filename tags.
8. The review brain checks story clarity, motion, identity, geometry, audio and originality before the next pass.

## Boundaries

The sources describe features and workflows at their stated dates. Availability, pricing, model names, generation lengths and regional access can change. The Markdown brains intentionally describe the production job first and use tool-specific features only as examples. They don't promise that an unverified route is available to every reader.

The award-winning story research in the course is used for transferable mechanisms such as a clear dramatic question, a small external engine, a strong relationship, a meaningful metaphor and one emotional turn. It isn't used to copy plots, characters, dialogue, protected visual identities or individual film styles.
