# Beginner First-Run Contract

DATE: 2026-09-04
STATUS: IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT, not proof of a completed generated film

A first-time learner must be able to complete these actions without already knowing AI terminology.

## Entry

1. Reach Lesson 1 as the primary course action.
2. Understand that the brain library is optional.
3. Find the Master brain and beginner guide before specialist cards.

## Brain activation

1. Learn that `.md` is plain text, not an app or plugin.
2. Use the brain inside a general-purpose AI chat.
3. Attach the `.md`, paste its complete text, or use an unchanged `.txt` copy when Markdown upload is unsupported.
4. Use the Master alone for a first film. Do not upload the full ZIP or all brains.
5. Receive a first response that confirms the brain name, states accessible materials and asks only the first useful question.

## Persistence

1. Save approved outputs outside the chat.
2. Keep one versioned Project State.
3. Reattach the Project State when a new chat begins.
4. Never assume old chat attachments persist.

## Minimum first film

1. Target 30–60 seconds.
2. Use one character, one location and one important prop.
3. Map the story into eight emotional beats and approximately six essential editorial shots.
4. Give every shot a stable `SHOT-##` identifier.
5. Cover the full timeline with approved clips or intentional still holds.
6. Make and approve the final image before motion production.

## Tool boundary

1. General AI chat: story, plans, prompts and QC.
2. Image generator: reference images and keyframes.
3. Video generator: motion clips from approved frames and prompts.
4. Audio source or tool: owned, generated under permitted terms or licensed sound.
5. Video editor: timeline assembly, sound and export.
6. Local storage: durable source files, prompts, results and approvals.

## Return bundle

Every generated image or clip returned to the AI chat includes:

```text
FILENAME:
SHOT ID:
TOOL / MODEL / DATE:
SOURCE REFERENCES:
EXACT PROMPT:
ACTUAL DURATION, IF VIDEO:
WHAT HAPPENED:
VISIBLE OR AUDIBLE DEFECT:
STATUS: PROPOSED / APPROVED / REJECTED
```

If the AI cannot inspect video, provide first, middle and last screenshots plus factual motion and audio notes.

## Final checks

1. A planning-only AI never claims it generated, edited or watched media.
2. Final film `PASS` requires inspection of playable picture and intended audio plus verified delivery facts.
3. The exported MP4 is reopened outside the editor and watched from start to finish.
4. Duration, aspect ratio, picture, intended audio, final-image hold and playback are confirmed.
5. References, likenesses, voices, music, effects, logos and borrowed material have known rights or are removed.
6. The course continues to label this as a researched and structurally tested system until a real beginner completes and verifies a playable film through the full route.
